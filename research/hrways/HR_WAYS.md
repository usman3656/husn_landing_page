# HR Ways — full site capture (content reference)

Captured 7 September 2026 from **https://hrways.co** (107 sitemap URLs plus the un-sitemapped `/recruitment/`, `/about-us/`, `/contact-us/`, `/faq/`, `/blog/`). Every page was downloaded and read. No screenshots were taken for this site (only requested for Founders Arm).

Note on the domain: **hrways.com** is a separate, parked "HRWAYS.COM — Contact us for any business inquiries" page with a bare contact form; it is not the company site. The FAQ on hrways.co even warns "info@hrways.co (not com)".

The site is WordPress (Astra child theme) built with **Elementor 4.2.2**, ElementsKit mega-menu, Yoast, and Seraphinite Accelerator. Several pages carry unfinished placeholders (e.g. `Info@localhost`, `jobs@localhost`, a "List Item #1/2/3" mega-menu block, a stray "http://localhost/hrways" link, and a `/services/` footer link that 404s).
