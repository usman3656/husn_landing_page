---
title: "Is Anxiety an Adverse Effect of TMS for Depression? A Retrospective Analysis"
description: "A retrospective chart analysis of 23 adults with major depressive disorder receiving high-frequency TMS found that while anxiety improved on average, 9 of 23 patients (39%) had higher anxiety scores post-treatment, suggesting clinicians should counsel patients about this possible adverse effect and identify predictors of who's at risk."
date: 2025-04-03
authors: "Morgan Healey, Lamaan Haq, Joshua Brown"
venue: "Journal of the Clinical TMS Society, Volume 3"
url: "https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S3050529125000406"
---
